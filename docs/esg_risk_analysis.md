# ENTERPRISE FRAMEWORK: ESG RISK ANALYSIS (esg_risk_analysis.md)

Instead of sentiment analysis, we evaluate society and corporate ecosystems as physical information systems.

## Aggregate States of Social Resilience:
- **State A (Stable/Apathetic):** High institutional trust or strong baseline discipline.
- **State B (Turbulent/Pre-Crisis):** Emerging hidden tensions, eroding trust, high sensitivity to external triggers.
- **State C (Chaotic/Systemic Failure):** Narrative collapse, breakdown of communication links, and loss of institutional control.

## Analytical Models for AI:
- **Epidemiological Information Model (SIR Model):** AI segments population/corporate sentiment into Susceptible ($S$), Infected ($I$), and Recovered ($R$) groups to calculate the reproduction coefficient of crisis-inducing information ($R_0$).
- **Cascading Failure Model:** Analysis of how micro-shocks trigger systemic collapses in corporate social networks.
