# ENTERPRISE FRAMEWORK: TECH INNOVATION RISK (tech_innovation_risk.md)

This framework protects the system from "hype-driven" investments and ensures innovation viability.

## Aggregate States of Innovation:
- **State A (Evolutionary):** Incremental growth and continuous improvement of existing IP.
- **State B (Disruptive):** Paradigm shift based on new technological breakthroughs.
- **State C (Regressive/Degradation):** Loss of technical chains due to resource gaps, brain drain, or infrastructure collapse.

## Analytical Models for AI:
- **S-Curve Innovation Model:** AI evaluates R&D expenditures vs. productivity returns to identify when an innovation reaches its plateau.
- **Critical Path Analysis (CPM/PERT):** Mapping technological dependencies to identify single points of failure in the manufacturing cycle.
