# ENTERPRISE FRAMEWORK: SUPPLY CHAIN SECURITY (supply_chain_security.md)

This ontology grounds AI agents on the physical reality of industrial operations. 
We analyze supply chain states based on material flow and infrastructure capacity.

## Aggregate States of Industrial Reality:
- **State A (Agile/Flow):** Optimized throughput, rapid response, high agility. Resource: Speed and Logistics leverage.
- **State B (Strain/Industrial):** Stress-testing production capacity. Resource: Throughput, buffer inventory, human labor availability.
- **State C (Fracture/Asymmetric):** Structural degradation. Resource: Resilience, alternative logistics, internal network integrity.

## Analytical Models for AI:
- **Bottleneck Theory (Goldratt’s TOC):** AI calculates the system's throughput capacity by identifying the weakest link in the supply chain.
- **Logistics Delta Analysis:** AI tracks lead-time variance between procurement tenders and actual material delivery to detect hidden bottlenecks.
